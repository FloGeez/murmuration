murmuration, a flock that draws shapes

Type to write, drag to draw

One flock for the whole page

With frame: 'page', a single canvas sits behind the content and the birds live in document coordinates. They perch on whatever a section offers them: here, a wire.

createMurmuration(canvas, { frame: 'page' })

A scene per section

createScenes picks the section on screen and lands the flock on its shape. Scroll back up: your word is still waiting.

createScenes(m, [{ name, active, pose }])

Shy of the pointer

With shy: true, perched birds take off when the pointer comes close, circle above their place, then land on it again. Try it on the ring.

m.perch(points, { shy: true })

Plain ES modules, no build, no dependency

npm i @flogeez/murmuration
